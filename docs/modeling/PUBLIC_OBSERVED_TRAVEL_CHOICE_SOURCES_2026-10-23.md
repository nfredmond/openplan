## What makes a source usable

A usable mode-choice observation joins a traveler and chosen mode to an origin, destination, date/time, purpose and relevant constraints. Estimation or conditional validation also needs travel conditions for **every available alternative**, including unchosen modes. Reported travel time or distance for the chosen mode alone does not meet that requirement. A geographic identifier enables a network join; it does not prove that the resulting times, fares, parking costs or access assumptions represent that traveler.

Trip diaries and primary tours answer different questions. This review treats trip records as potential inputs to a documented tour reconstruction, not ready-made tour-mode outcomes. It also distinguishes public-file geography from coordinates collected privately by a survey. Coarsened, shifted or suppressed endpoints can prevent a WALK or transit-access test even when a regional auto-mode test remains possible.

**DRAFT ONLY. NOT A PREREGISTRATION. NOT AUTHORIZATION TO ACQUIRE OUTCOMES OR RUN A MODEL.** The lead must resolve every admission item and commit a separate frozen protocol before execution. This outline proposes regional development evidence. It cannot close S2 or S3, accept a nationwide coefficient package, or revive the retired SEMCOG transfer.

### Question, hypothesis and component

Provisionally use the TBI 2019 public release for the Twin Cities region to ask whether ActivitySim assigns calibrated probabilities to observed **primary-tour modes**, conditional on observed travelers, destinations and schedules. The primary hypothesis is that one separately justified, frozen candidate improves weighted multiclass log loss relative to the frozen current ActivitySim baseline. No candidate is selected here. A baseline-only first stage can diagnose errors but cannot claim an improvement or production acceptance.

Start with individual, complete, home-based tours. Reconstruct them from ordered diary trips using an explicit primary-activity and tour-mode hierarchy. Preserve raw codes and all exclusion reasons. Do not label a trip's mode as a tour's mode without this mapping. Treat joint tours, mixed-mode chains and at-work subtours separately until their participant and vehicle-availability mappings pass admission. Keep school and university distinct; an adult school-purpose trip does not by itself establish university status. If the public source cannot distinguish them, the corresponding question remains inconclusive.

### Admission before opening outcomes

1. **Authority:** pin survey release, public links, terms and hashes. Confirm zero-cost access and intended analysis/publication rights. A metadata field or an unexecuted agreement cannot establish approval. Do not acquire restricted geography or infer suppressed locations.
2. **Custody:** audit prior access by all known OpenPlan studies and agents. Preserve every consumed NHTS division and the reserved 2017/2009 records. Do not reuse a regional survey wave already used to fit the baseline as an independent transfer sample. Record any donor/model overlap.
3. **Travel conditions:** obtain free, authorized, survey-year road, pedestrian, bicycle and transit networks, transit timetables, fares, tolls, parking assumptions and congestion periods. [VERIFY: availability, rights and historic coverage for each input.] Pin walk/bicycle access rules, intrazonal treatment, time zones, overnight trips, currency year and value-of-time inputs. Current conditions are not a substitute for survey-year conditions without a declared sensitivity design.
4. **Geography:** establish the actual public OD unit, boundary vintage and displacement/suppression. Tract or TAZ centroids are approximations. Before residuals, grade within-zone travel, station access and short walk trips. Use a mode-independent spatial sensitivity method fixed in advance; never choose a point within a zone because it explains the observed mode. If geography cannot discriminate plausible alternatives, report an inadmissible observation rather than a measured zero error.
5. **Choice availability:** build all feasible alternatives, including unchosen ones, without using the chosen mode to invent feasibility. Household vehicles, licensing, transit operating hours and round-trip vehicle continuity matter. Do not disable transit because a feed is missing. Missing alternative conditions fail admission; true service unavailability is a separate state.
6. **Survey design:** select the correct person-day or tour weight with the publisher's guidance. Do not average inconsistent trip weights into a tour weight. Freeze household clustering, strata, day normalization, panels, nonresponse and replicate/Taylor/bootstrap method. [VERIFY: source-supported tour-weight interpretation and variance method.] A simulated-choice envelope does not measure survey uncertainty.
