# Planning context and handoffs

This is the current boundary guide for connecting existing modules. It records
dated September 4 source findings, reconciled with the September 6 developer handoff. It does not announce new implementation. The
[architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) maps components, the
[capability matrix](US_PLANNING_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) records incomplete jobs,
and the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) specifies repairs and their verification.

## One case, distinct facts

Projects and statutory plans are the intended owners of the planning case.
Workspace home is a default context, not the client's legal authority. Exact
study geometry, administrative identity, adopting/reviewing bodies, applicable
law and its effective version need separate records and responsible human review.

The current land-use create route restricts configured bundles by workspace home.
That prevents one inappropriate selection but rejects valid cross-client work
and cannot prove applicability to the plan itself. California general and
specific plans also share requirements that need separate plan-kind rules.
These are open defects; a neutral workflow's disclaimer does not repair them.
Newer section applicability and draft-save corrections do not change these authority rules. See the [land-use product contract](LAND_USE_PLANS_CONTRACT.md) and the dated [product review](../reviews/2026-09-04-pre-handoff/independent-product.md).

Generic Plans, Land Use Plans and RTP have different existing responsibilities.
Choose the home that actually owns the job; do not interpret linked-record
presence checks as a legal completeness determination. Consolidate repeated
context and evidence handling after a real case exposes the correct boundary.

## Import and reuse

GIS imports need CRS/axis order, geometry, identity, units, source vintage,
license and completeness. A successful parse or visible footprint does not
establish the complete dataset. Source caps must remain attached to every derived
total, severity count, score and narrative. An unavailable total is not zero.

Portfolio workbooks currently support reviewed **creation** and skips. They are
not a reviewed update/merge operation for existing records, and every exported
structured place field is not restored automatically. Retain that conservative
contract until identity-based reconciliation and its conflict preview are built.

Direct project GeoPackages contain project/corridor geometry. Governed evidence
packages add selected approved records, including additional safety/engagement
evidence. The current generator uses the newest reconciled project crash acquisition and retains earlier acquisitions as evidence records. Existing builder support for model-link and land-use-designation layers
is not supplied by those production call sites. Export names must not imply
those layers are present. Reuse requires inspecting actual layers/rows in an
independent GIS or spreadsheet tool, not merely downloading a file.

## Sources, review and public records

Documents and GIS own source inputs and their versions. Analysis, models and
safety produce evidence. Reports and governed packages select that evidence.
An update can invalidate a current conclusion without rewriting a frozen adopted
or approved record. Preserve the source and exact version at each handoff.

Engagement contributions need receipt, moderation status, agency disposition and
decision linkage. Participation counts do not establish a representative sample.
Public derivatives must preserve relevant caveats while excluding confidential
originals. Workspace RLS is a foundation; case confidentiality, records holds,
retention and tribal data control are not established by that boundary alone.

Implementation and funding records need named responsibility, due dates, status,
authorization and amount/price-year basis. Plan adoption is not project delivery;
an award is not reimbursement. Carry changes through the existing project,
program, grant and invoice records rather than re-entering unbound copies.

## Evidence for a completed handoff

Observe one planner's actual job and the recipient's reuse. Start at visible
navigation, retain candidate/deployment identity, test desktop and 390px plus
keyboard access, inspect the console and the stored/exported artifact, and test
an interrupted or rejected step. A second person must understand what the record
means and what remains unsupported. Agent journeys help expose regressions;
they cannot establish professional usefulness, legal sufficiency or public trust.

## Early priority handoffs: engagement, agents and capital delivery

Roadmap M9/A0/M10 makes these connected upgrades early work. The public record must retain the question/version, source contribution and receipt through moderation, agency response and a particular approved decision. For adopted commitments, link the exact plan/project version to implementation responsibility and status; unanswered or rejected recommendations remain distinguishable.

A capital case continues through environmental/ROW/utilities/PS&E, procurement, construction and closeout. Preserve responsible authorities, phase authorizations, changes and source documents. Contractor payments, consultant invoices and funder reimbursement are linked but distinct ledgers; a paid invoice does not by itself establish eligibility or close a project. Mid-construction intake must disclose missing earlier records instead of inventing a completed planning history.

The Planner Agent may use the user's selected API or supported installed CLI once A0 is implemented. Provider choice changes execution and account ownership, not the authoritative case. Grounded drafts and proposed actions still pass through existing permissions, evidence and exact approvals. Personal CLI access and unattended public/agency AI are separately configured; no implicit account pooling.

## Planning contracts and RTP updates

M11 connects an executed planning agreement to tasks, staff allocations, actual time/cost, deliverables, remaining-work estimates and delivery forecasts. Invoice presentation may group work differently, but it must preserve source allocations and approved rates. Internal costs, gross fees, retention, cash and funder claims retain distinct meanings. Baseline amendments require human authority; changing a forecast does not change the contract.

M12 begins an RTP update with the prior adopted plan in Documents. Preserve source pages and coverage, explicitly review figures/project matches and chapter text, and retain old/new differences. The regional plan owns its policy/action/financial elements and adoption history; engagement responses and implementation obligations link to specific plan versions. Its regional funding tables are separate from the consultant's contract budget. Ordinary chapter extraction is currently disconnected, so the documented intake journey is not established as complete.

## Municipal reporting to an administering agency

Programs/local_measure owns the administered fund; recipient municipalities need scoped participation on the hosting instance. Project outputs and expenditure evidence link to a versioned reporting period and exact governing agreement. Submission, certification, review acceptance, disbursement/reimbursement and public release are separate actions. Shared hosting does not grant cities each other's private records or fund-wide payment authority.

Use source-defined project/segment quantities with units and period/cumulative basis. One physical improvement may support several funding reports but is counted once in an aggregate with a stated attribution method. Preserve no-activity, missing and disputed records. Formula distributions and grant reimbursements follow their actual rules; successor measures retain original obligations, applicable tax periods and approved transfer history. M13 supplies this currently incomplete workflow.

## Procurement into contracted planning and construction work

M14 connects the agency's need and funding authority to an approved solicitation, while a separate consultant pursuit owns its private response. Issued versions, Q&A/addenda, exact submitted packages, receipt, permitted evaluation and award belong to distinct records. Shared public references do not grant cross-firm or agency access to private drafts, estimates, fee material or panel work. Apply required cost concealment through every derivative and assistant context.

An authorized human publishes/submits; external delivery remains unconfirmed without supporting receipt. Selection and intent to award are not execution or notice to proceed. Once executed, approved tasks, staff/rates, deliverables, ceiling, funding and deadlines populate M11/M10 with differences from the proposal retained. Grant applications and subrecipient administration remain separate workflows even where documents and contacts are shared.

## Current acceptance and tailored participation records

The [final first-week record](../ops/V044_FINAL_FIRST_WEEK_OUTCOMES_2026-09-06.md)
records nine passes and three partial outcomes. Separate draft, custody, GIS and
artifact corrections retain their own evidence. No complete current release or
whole-product acceptance follows from this boundary guide. Source-level findings
remain dated until the specified behavior is directly rechecked.

A planner must be able to start a project-specific public map, tailor categories,
questions, instructions, study area and layers, and review the public version.
Moderation must preserve the original meaning and separate receipt, public
release and agency response. PDF and typed/filterable XLSX exports must identify
their exact snapshot, filters and audience. Public derivatives must exclude
pending/private content in every sheet, attachment and metadata field. Current
CSV/report foundations do not establish this complete required handoff.

## OWP and UPWP administration

The agency program owns its adopted cycle, work elements, funding vintages,
authorization periods, amendments and carryover. Projects and work plans provide
actual work products and responsibilities. Contract/time records supply eligible
source costs; invoices, paid cash, claims and certifications remain distinct.
A successor program must inherit evidenced unfinished obligations without
rewriting the prior adopted or closed cycle. The complete preparation-to-closeout
job remains unassessed. Use the [OWP research](../reviews/2026-09-04-pre-handoff/OWP_ADMINISTRATION_REQUIREMENTS_RESEARCH.md)
and CORE-OWP-01 for California MPO/RTPA and contrasting non-California cases.

## Aerial evidence and customer ownership

Aerial work carries mission settings, aircraft/controller profile, original
photos, control/CRS/vertical-unit evidence, processing version, full output
inventory and use limits into the project. Generic KML review geometry, supported
DJI mission export, USB copy, controller import and an authorized flight each
require distinct evidence. ODM/WebODM capability and generated output alone do
not establish OpenPlan integration, accepted accuracy or superiority.

A hosted trial must carry one customer's permitted records, source/artifact
bytes and role/approval history into an independently operated installation.
Provider credentials and recipient access require explicit reauthorization.
Optional implementation/administration services do not change free software access
or grant the operator authority over adoption, publication, money or scientific
claims. Public commissioning, complete portability and independent recovery
remain required outcomes, not established behavior.
