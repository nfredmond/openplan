## What is proved

- A member can freeze one immutable `project_evidence_manifest.v2` package,
  bind it to its exact bundle SHA-256, and submit it to a different owner/admin.
- Only the named approver can return or approve the exact submitted hash.
  The bundle creator, submitter, unassigned administrators, viewers, hash
  mismatches, and cross-workspace callers are refused.
- Return requires a reason and a replacement submission with a different bundle
  hash. Decisions and submissions are append-only.
- Approval creates a separate immutable receipt and does not mutate the ZIP,
  publish it, assert statutory adoption, or assert model validation.
- Current project, linked-plan, and evidence inventory revisions are checked at
  submit and decision time. A changed or unreadable source fails closed and
  requires a new bundle.
- The package's manifest, report output, and GeoPackage layer status use the
  shared point-of-use evidence descriptor. Unsupported numeric evidence cannot
  enter the approvable path.
