# OWP reporting and reimbursement

Open **Programming Cycles**, choose the program, and open **Administer a reporting period**. Owners and administrators can use **Reimbursement packets** below the internal period reports.

1. Record and approve the source staff effort and expenses in the existing source register. For shared contract costs, choose the existing OWP valuation in the contract source list. Use the same physical time/spending entry and currency. Correct the OWP source first and reconcile its current approved contract valuation; do not enter another expense for its reimbursement.
2. Record dated completed products, outstanding work and the basis for remaining-work estimates in the reporting period. Unknown estimates stay unknown. Review and issue the internal report against the adopted baseline.
3. Start a reimbursement draft from that report. Enter the funding agreement/authority reference and the applicable forms, versions and supporting requirements you reviewed. Give every incurred cost an eligibility amount and reason, including zero for excluded costs. Allocate the eligible amount across the baseline funds as reimbursement or match, with evidence for each share. Fund dates must cover the source cost.
4. Save the draft, enter the finance review evidence, then freeze the reviewed packet. The packet retains the internal report/hash, exact source versions, contract valuations, funding decisions, work progress and linked deliverable records. Prepare PDF and XLSX, check their worker status, and download the retained files.
5. After an actual external action, record its submission receipt, return or acceptance evidence. These controls record evidence; they do not send anything, pay an invoice or grant authority.
6. When returned, correct eligibility/shares and the explanation, save, and review again. If source amounts or progress changed, first correct/reissue the internal report and select its corrected version. Reconcile shared contract sources too. Then prepare the new packet and record its resubmission receipt. Earlier files and receipts remain available. Only the current request counts.

An interrupted save retains its exact request on this browser. Use **Retry packet save** before making another change. The server returns the original result for that same request, including when the first response was lost. Select the saved packet after reopening the page to continue.

## Boundaries

This is a private supporting packet. Its references do not certify that costs are eligible or that an agency's prescribed form is complete. The responsible reviewer must check actual agreements, cost/payment requirements, form versions and attachments. No jurisdiction's rules are inferred from another jurisdiction, and no universal filing-compatible template is claimed.

Each physical source cost is reserved to one packet lineage in its workspace, including after return and even when currently excluded. Split eligible funding across funds within that packet. Separate packets cannot divide or reclaim the same source; there is no automatic reservation release or transfer. Corrections retain the reporting period. Accepted packets cannot be rewritten through this cycle; subsequent authorized reopening/closeout remains M2d.4 work.

Amounts are exact cents. Incurred costs, reimbursement requests, match, billing and cash remain separate. Source completeness is bounded by the retained register; unrecorded costs are not discovered automatically. Dated progress can explicitly report unassessed remaining effort/cost. Deliverable evidence is frozen at packet review; it does not imply product acceptance.
