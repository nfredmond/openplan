## Corrected capability

Three forward-only migrations finish the custody and governance boundary:

- `20260827000001_model_truth_completion.sql` binds each guided comparison to
  exactly four completed assignment outputs: AequilibraE baseline and build,
  plus ActivitySim baseline and build. Deterministic latest artifacts, exact
  artifact hashes, shared network and assignment digests, current build
  assumptions, and track-matched claim decisions must all agree. An
  ActivitySim preflight or unavailable assignment remains visibly distinct
  from an assigned-volume result.
- `20260827000002_governed_decision_enforcement.sql` enforces the current
  complete manifest, different authorized approver, exact submitted bundle
  identity, immutable rows, and one disposition per submission in the
  database. The submission and approval routes separately read the private
  Storage object and verify its exact bytes. Approval stores canonical receipt
  bytes and the SHA-256 of those same bytes.
- `20260827000003_evidence_dependency_freshness.sql` makes a package stale when
  its project, selected plan, report, or other bound evidence revision changes.
  A stale submission can still be returned, but cannot be approved.

Evidence descriptors are now validated when runtime records are read and again
at freeze. Generated evidence binds its checksum, retrieval time, revision, and
authenticated generator to the exact rendered or source bytes.

The GeoPackage exports only geometry from approved public comments in campaigns
with a live public page. It excludes comment text, submitter identity,
moderation notes, and private records. The renderer can preserve separately
supplied AequilibraE links, ActivitySim links, and land-use designations. The
current bundle generator does not yet supply those upstream geometries, so the
layer-status table labels them unavailable instead of inventing, merging, or
silently omitting them.

My Work remains scoped to one active workspace. A caller-specific RLS probe now
finds only assigned work waiting in another membership, shows a notice, and
offers a direct membership-checked switch to that exact review. Evidence-bundle
history now shows and copies the complete 64-character manifest SHA-256 on
desktop and at 390 px.

## Failures found during verification

Verification found four real defects after the prior completion claim:

- Archive creation initially returned a database `409` because the generated
  descriptor's revision token did not match the revision validated at freeze.
  The descriptor is now rebound to the archived bytes, with regression
  coverage.
- Prior-bundle history shortened the manifest SHA-256. It now exposes all 64
  characters and copies the exact value.
- An approver whose active workspace differed from the assigned package saw an
  empty My Work queue. The caller-RLS notice and direct switch now make the
  package reachable without guessing.
- The first deterministic rerun compared a mixed-case generated workspace name
  against the database's normalized lowercase name. That was a harness defect,
  not a product defect; the assertion now follows the stored normalization and
  the complete rerun passed.

The final first-week runs found no additional product issue. Job 09 correctly
reported that its browser sandbox had no archive or GIS reader; that is a
harness limit, and the independent repository verifier below supplies the
required archive and GeoPackage evidence.
