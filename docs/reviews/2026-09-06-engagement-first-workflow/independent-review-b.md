## Answers to the ten required questions

1. **What would make this the ultimate free planning operating system?** A planner should carry one real case from governing authority and adopted predecessor through evidence, participation, decisions, funding, delivery, recurring reporting and transferable records. Staff should see who owns each unresolved item and resume after interruption without re-entering facts. The same system must work without Nathaniel, paid feature gates or compulsory AI. Complete Engagement is valuable because it joins residents' evidence to that enduring case.

2. **Can every type of US planner do their core work here?** No. The capability matrix documents partial or unassessed work, including complete RTP cycles, land-use authority and housing/development review, environmental and mitigation practice, modal investment decisions, grant pursuit, procurement, full capital administration, OWP adoption/reporting/closeout, planning-contract forecasts and local-tax recipient administration. Complete shared-file/OCR intake, human document work, aerial operations, installed/API agent choice and customer-owned deployment remain explicit obligations. These findings do not establish that each subfeature is absent. They establish that complete practice has not been proved. Departmental intake, hearing records, conditions and implementation should remain visible even where the current module map lacks a familiar name.

3. **Does every state work in substance?** Not established. The maintained matrix explicitly has no proven dimension entries and identifies incomplete grant/state procedural depth. Generic campaigns, map extents and truthful unsupported states cannot prove 51 substantive statewide workflows. Add jurisdiction/source-specific evidence as actual cases advance. Preserve explicit territory assessment, sovereign/overlapping authorities, rural and island cases. This review performed no new nationwide legal or source-currentness audit.

4. **Is California the gold standard?** No evidence supports that claim. Source records identify incomplete plan-owned authority, local/coastal/tribal applicability, capital/reimbursement work and multiple incomplete planning journeys. The two requested engagement contexts are useful variation within California, but cannot stand for all counties, incorporated places, MPO/RTPA arrangements, tribal governments or environmental/land-use regimes. Keep California's deeper obligation intact.

5. **Are both travel models validated for every published use in every state?** No. The contract, architecture and matrix retain no independent nationwide accuracy result. The dated ActivitySim runtime record establishes execution while explicitly discussing borrowed behavioral parameters and mode/period limitations. Execution is separate from scientific validity. Keep AequilibraE and ActivitySim separate, preserve inconclusive studies and consumed holdouts, correct structure using development evidence, then evaluate untouched use-specific geographic acceptance. Engagement counts or model agreement cannot repair this gap.

6. **Simplest overlooked idea with largest effect?** Make the return-to-work and recipient questions first-class: "What was retained, what changed, what still needs my decision, and where is its exact evidence?" Reuse existing receipts, review history, Reports and My Work. The concrete first step is a readable campaign review with question denominators and traceable continuation pages, accompanied by the complete register. This reduces the agency's reconstruction work without inventing an AI summary or another dashboard.

7. **Which old rule or decision is wrong?** Treating a successful PDF/XLSX job, count reconciliation or a dense register as proof that a recipient can use the report is wrong. The current roadmap already says otherwise. Likewise, completing M9a engineering cannot close M9c comparative superiority. No evidence from this review justifies narrowing v1 or restarting completed OWP preparation. A packet tied to a commit plus dirty substantive changes must not be described as a final identified release review.

8. **What should be removed, joined or deepened?** Join photo/retry/draft identity, map/GeoJSON normalization and participant-language recovery within their existing shared helpers. Deepen historical definitions, scoped reporting and readable survey tables using the existing snapshot. Keep restricted originals under staff controls; do not copy them into public reports for apparent completeness. Join reviewed input to project alternatives and accountable staff response under M9b. No new top-level module is warranted.

9. **What would falsify the recommendation?** For each defect, demonstrate its described counterexample passing on the final identified build and the corresponding targeted regression failing when deliberately reintroduced. For reporting, independently observed recipients must find a specified historical contribution, determine what question/options it answered, explain missing/repeated records, locate its map geometry and read the corresponding response without repair or developer coaching. If current artifacts already support those tasks reliably, the report redesign recommendation should narrow. Superiority requires a preregistered, lawful comparison against current Social Pinpoint with matched tasks and observed participants; that evidence could contradict the proposed priorities. A complete independently restored campaign archive containing retained originals/history would overturn the archive limitation for that demonstrated export path.

10. **Highest-leverage next completed outcome from v1?** Finish the authorized two-context Engagement engineering journey with trustworthy recovery and reconciled, readable, downloadable files. Keep its result explicitly bounded. Then run the observed planner/participant and recipient exercises and carry one reviewed concern into a documented project decision and public explanation under M9b.
