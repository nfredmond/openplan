## Perspectives covered

use-specific acceptance and actual multimodal/safety decisions. Land-use and
statutory practice needs plan-owned applicability, full RTP/land-use documents and
development decisions. Environmental/climate/resilience/equity work requires
specialist-reviewed complete cases beyond generated narrative. Engagement/Title VI
requires participation access, receipt/moderation/response distinctions and actual
public/internal PDF/XLSX artifact reuse. Capital/grants/reimbursement needs explicit
cost/fee/cash/authority lineage and valid underspend/closeout.

Rural and tribal organizations need distinct observed cases, sovereign authority,
data control and constrained operation. GIS/public records needs exact selected
geometry, sources, records holds and derivative permissions. Agency operations
needs independent installation/recovery, case-level delegation and accessible
human workflows. The adversarial product question is whether broad module labels
still conceal these complete jobs. The expanded ledger is necessary but cannot
answer that without real outcomes.
