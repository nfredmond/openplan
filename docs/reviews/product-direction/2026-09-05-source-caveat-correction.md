The September 5 milestone decision remains binding. The two independent contexts
are the same preserved reports, not new reviews of this subsequent patch.
Reviewer A's multi-project engagement-evidence continuity hypothesis and
reviewer B's plan-to-assigned-work and completion-evidence hypothesis remain
separate. After the release gate, visibly test A first; if falsified, test B.
Neither hypothesis authorizes a new module or a reduced v1 scope.

The latest browser journey and independently downloaded nine-page packet prove
the severe-crash count and ranking correction on `efd4eb9b`. They also exposed
an incorrect shared source-description sentence. The new patch removes that
unsupported coverage claim and adds the existing fatality-census disclosure
only for a recorded fatal-only source. Focused tests and restored mutations
establish its behavior. Fresh browser verification and the complete twelve-job
first-week outcome gate remain outstanding. Lesser recoverable save timing and
print pagination issues are recorded rather than silently called fixed.

This is still a modeling and interoperability release with corrective
first-week work, not calibration, nationwide validation, California completeness,
or v1 readiness. The failed distributed-loading candidate stays retired and
inconclusive. Defaults, acceptance rules, source bytes, networks, frozen
studies, and holdouts remain unchanged. No replacement is fitted from failure.

The whole-product destination remains all planner types across all 50 states
and DC, California as the gold standard across its diversity, nationally
validated separate AequilibraE and ActivitySim methods, self-service operation
without paid infrastructure, and human control of consequential decisions.
Runtime, release count, and convenience do not reduce that destination.
