reviewer B proposes testing plan-to-assigned-work and completion evidence.

supported. Do not create a parallel module or narrow the binding v1 target.

invented to obtain a yes. Lesser findings enter the capability registry.

audit reports zero; dependency maintenance remains visible.

This remains release correction, not model tuning or a new product lane.

holdouts, method separation, defaults, and acceptance rules are unchanged.
No current evidence establishes calibration, California completeness,
nationwide validation, or v1 readiness. A complete first-week outcome gate
remains required before tagging v0.44.
