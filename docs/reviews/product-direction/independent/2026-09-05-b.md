## All ten protocol questions

### 1. What would make this the ultimate free planning operating system?

A planner should carry a public problem through evidence, alternatives, a responsible human decision, implementation, and later review without reconstructing the record at each handoff. The same record must work for a small tribal government, a city department, an MPO, a state agency, and a consultant's client. Sources, geography, legal applicability, dissent, missing evidence, approvals, and revisions must travel with the work. A stranger must be able to operate and recover the installation. The binding all-state and scientifically validated dual-model requirements stay intact.

The destination is broader than a large module inventory or another isolated model experiment. The contract in `docs/product/V1_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` already expresses it well; I recommend no scope reduction.

### 2. Can every type of US planner do their core work?

No. The capability registry marks environmental/resilience and development-review planners `not-assessed`; state agencies, tribal governments, and community/independent users are also unproven. Missing proof is not proof that no code exists. There are climate, hazard, environmental review, zoning, annexation, housing, and transit work-plan templates, and land-use implementation actions already exist. Templates alone do not establish a finished planning job.

Shallow or unproven jobs include statutory plan cycles, development application review and conditions, environmental alternatives and mitigation follow-through, transit service-to-capital decisions, complete injury analysis, public-records lifecycle, and project funding through closeout. Interdepartmental implementation is especially concrete: the stored assignment and evidence fields are stronger than the visible workbench controls described below. Sources are the capability registry/matrix, `openplan/src/lib/work-plans/templates/`, and land-use contracts, API, workbench, and My Work sources.

### 3. Does every state work in substance?

No. The capability registry marks California partial and other state/DC cells not assessed. The job-by-jurisdiction readiness system has three documented exemplars, California, Oregon, and Puerto Rico. Honest fatal-only national crash coverage does not supply serious-injury coverage or state statutory depth. Neutral land-use workflows preserve records but explicitly do not determine applicable law. No national source should promote all state cells.

### 4. Is California the gold standard across geography and agency types?

No. Its configured land-use descriptor explicitly disclaims complete legal sufficiency. There is no demonstrated complete California statewide, metropolitan, rural, border, coastal, mountain, and tribal acceptance set. County models remain scientifically inconclusive. Small-agency installation and stranger reuse remain open. California is the deepest implementation, which is different from having met the contract.

### 5. Are both models scientifically validated for every published use everywhere?

No. The known-issues record retains an unknown model base year, synthetic expanded daily traffic instead of proven AADT comparability, no frozen use-specific acceptance rule, and borrowed ActivitySim coefficients. The distributed-loading candidate failed its whole-candidate advancement rule. The stock runtime record also describes absent represented transit and repeated free-flow skims across time periods; it is dated evidence that must be remeasured before quoting numerical shares today.

The scientific next step must explain the registered reach and road-class failures using frozen development evidence, then specify a source-supported structural correction and independent acceptance design before a new candidate. LODES work locations do not support non-work through traffic. Do not tune a scalar, cherry-pick advanced county/method cells, rematch observations after seeing residuals, open an untouched holdout, or change defaults from these results.

Current primary ActivitySim documentation still distinguishes donor examples from complete calibrated agency models. That supports maintaining the distinction between software execution and local validation: [ActivitySim documentation](https://activitysim.github.io/activitysim/develop/index.html). This narrow source check is not a new exhaustive engine review.

### 6. What is the simplest overlooked idea with the largest effect?

Make an adopted plan's commitment become visible assigned work, and let a second person return completion evidence into the same plan record.

This does not require inventing a task system. `openplan/src/app/api/land-use-plans/[planId]/implementation/route.ts` already accepts `assigneeUserId`, `evidenceDocumentId`, and linked project/program identities. `openplan/src/lib/my-work/sources.ts` already reads those assigned land-use actions. But the implementation form in `openplan/src/components/land-use-plans/land-use-plan-workbench.tsx:267` exposes a free-text responsible party, due date, project, and program. Existing action rows show title, party, date, and a status control. They do not display the saved project/program links as navigation or expose the assignment/evidence fields there.

That is a source-backed gap in this component, not a browser-proven claim that no other route supplies the function. The large product effect would be making Plans, My Work, Projects, Programs, Documents, and progress reporting one usable handoff. It directly advances the currently unassessed implementation/interdepartmental need.
