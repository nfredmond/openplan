### 6. What is the simplest overlooked idea with the largest product effect?

Assigned work should remain discoverable when nobody has set its deadline yet.

There is a specific source-level omission. `openplan/src/app/api/land-use-plans/[planId]/implementation/route.ts` accepts an optional nullable `dueOn` independently of `assigneeUserId`. `openplan/src/lib/my-work/sources.ts:728` reads plan actions with a `notNull` filter on `due_on`. The exported `MY_WORK_SOURCES` list contains that one plan-action reader. The existing undated source reads project issues, not plan actions. Thus a legitimate assigned plan action without a deadline is outside the current My Work query's scope. An active, dated action is already supported, so creating another task system would duplicate existing machinery.

This is source evidence of exclusion, not a fresh browser reproduction. The product hypothesis is that it causes staff handoffs to disappear from the place they are told to find their work. Prove that with a manager and assignee before implementing. Keep absence of a deadline explicit; do not invent dates to make records appear.
