Why coherence comes first

1

The interface exposes modules, not one planning job

2

Completed automation still records partly reached outcomes

The latest first-week evidence reaches the end of scripts while safety locations remain coordinates and the printable packet lacks street context.

External handoff and team responsibility remain incomplete

GIS/workbook round-trip, evidence manifests, role coverage, named approval, and one cross-module inbox are necessary for agency work.

Five tests for the contract

Self-service

Install, operate, recover, and upgrade without founder help or paid infrastructure.

One product

Projects and plans retain context across every module transition.

Any place

US-wide today, with country-specific systems kept behind adapters.

Outcomes

Only reached passes. Completed-plus-partly fails.

Defensible handoff

Source, tier, limits, responsible human, and frozen evidence travel with the result.
