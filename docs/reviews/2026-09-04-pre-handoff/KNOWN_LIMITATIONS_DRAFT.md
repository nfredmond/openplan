## How to read this register

**Source-established** means the relevant implementation was traced; it is not a runtime reproduction. **Historical observation** means the dated journey cited in the existing register found the problem; current persistence or closure needs current evidence. **Parent review** identifies findings owned by the concurrent engineering review. **Not assessed** means no conclusion of absence or successful behavior is justified.

Keep four dispositions separate: disclosure repaired, diagnosis completed, behavior corrected, and accepted for a specified use. A better warning does not repair missing data; a diagnosis does not correct demand/network structure; an executed model does not establish scientific accuracy. Preserve historical records and negative results when a limitation is closed.
