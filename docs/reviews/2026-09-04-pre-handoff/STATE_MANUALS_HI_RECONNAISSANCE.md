# Hawaii local-assistance manuals: reconnaissance

September 4, 2026, Pacific time. This is a bounded primary-source reconnaissance, not a complete manual review or legal determination. Hawaii belongs within the existing all-50-states-and-DC v1 commitment. Its county delivery arrangements, island geography and dated public manual offer useful tests of whether OpenPlan can represent actual authority rather than copy California terminology.

## Authority and currency come before automation

The 2020 manual remains a useful workflow source. It describes county delivery, phase authorization before eligible expenditure, reimbursement review, consultant approvals and separate construction acceptance/federal closeout. Chapters 4 and 9 describe supporting payment records, cumulative reimbursement, claims resolution, final estimates and remaining right-of-way/utility obligations. However, its fixed reimbursement schedules and retention references require current-authority reconciliation before becoming automatic deadlines. [Manual pp.7–12, 41–44, 57–58, 65–73](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/highways/files/2021/11/LPA-Manual-Rev_20201013.pdf).

Two publication defects matter. The manual says the State–County MOA is included in full, but retrieved Appendix B contains only its title page; Appendix A is also only a title page. Both were visually inspected and the PDF reports no embedded files. The manual also retains a description of Maui MPO as being created and Oahu as the only MPO as of 2014. These are reasons to qualify this edition, not proof that operative agreements are unavailable elsewhere. [Manual pp.11, 19–20, 78–79](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/highways/files/2021/11/LPA-Manual-Rev_20201013.pdf).

The 2024 stewardship agreement is the stronger current source for delegated federal actions. It expressly makes Attachment A controlling for that delegation. Its selected rows distinguish FHWA obligation/modification/closure and reimbursement authorization from state advertisement and award concurrence. Consultant management-support approval remains federal, while the table assigns noncompetitive engineering/design procurement approval to the state. Project-specific agreements may change delegated actions; state action alone does not establish federal funding eligibility. Agency staffing, accounting and delivery risk also matter. Therefore an old manual's dollar-based oversight classification cannot populate a universal approval route. [Agreement §§I–VII, Attachment A pp.9–10, 13](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/federalaid/stewardship/agreements/hi.pdf).

A usable source hierarchy should retain applicable law and award conditions, the current federal/state delegation agreement, executed county/project agreements, current HDOT procedures and dated updates. A public manual is evidence within that hierarchy. OpenPlan should surface conflicts and missing documents for an authorized reviewer rather than silently select whichever PDF was imported last. That is a design recommendation, not a claim that this review settled every precedence question.

## Hawaii-specific workflow differences

**Agency and island are different records.** Current Maui MPO describes its planning area as the island of Maui and says it does not directly construct projects. The current STIP page recognizes both Oahu and Maui MPOs. Thus Maui County cannot automatically equal Maui MPO jurisdiction or construction delivery responsibility. Molokai and Lanai provide valuable boundary cases; exact current adopted planning-area geometry remains unreviewed. [Maui MPO](https://www.mauimpo.org/), [HDOT STIP](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/highways/other/other-related-links/stip/).

HDOT separately publishes 2025 Federal-Aid Urban Boundary and functional-classification updates, with policy approvals dated May 9 and June 25 respectively. Those classifications must not be inferred from a county, Census urban area or workspace name. [HDOT classification updates](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/highways/other/federal-aid-highways-and-state-highway-system-update/). This supports typed county, island, MPO, road-owner and eligibility records, with project geometry and authoritative boundaries as evidence.

**Procurement needs the service and funding basis.** The published consultant procedure covers federally funded engineering/design services related to construction. It distinguishes payment methods, independent estimation, ranked negotiation, amendments and confidentiality. It also contains a historical $150,000 small-purchase threshold and references the 2015 stewardship agreement. Neither should become an asserted current default without reconciliation. The useful reusable structure is qualifications evidence, approved procurement method, estimate/cost separation, negotiation history, conflict review and scope-controlled amendments. It does not establish that every planning RFP must use this procedure. [Procedure PDF pp.1–9](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/highways/files/2022/04/HDOT-Engineering-and-Design-Related-Services-Procedures-5-19-16_rev-09-09-2016.pdf).

**Civil-rights obligations cannot be switched off together.** The current DBE page says new contract goals are suspended from the October 3, 2025 trigger until reevaluation finishes; it preserves prevailing wage, certified payroll and prompt payment. The page also lists proposed future goals. A proposal or elapsed application deadline is not evidence that goals resumed. This review did not establish completion of the statewide process. Preserve Title VI and ADA as separate programs and refresh the actual applicable DBE direction when preparing a solicitation. [HDOT DBE notices](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/administration/ocr/dbe/).

**The highway manual is not the entire modal framework.** HDOT identifies a separate State Management Plan for FTA Sections 5310, 5311 and 5339, administered through its statewide transportation planning office. A transit grant should not inherit highway phase, form and approval rules solely because the project is in Hawaii. [State Management Plan](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/administration/stp-2/state-management-plan/), [FTA grant programs](https://hidot.hawaii.gov/administration/fta-grant-programs/).

## Existing roadmap homes and proof needed

These are proposed acceptance refinements to the existing roadmap. This reconnaissance did not inspect application implementation and does not establish code absence, a working integration or browser acceptance.

| Existing home | Concrete addition and falsification evidence |
|---|---|
| M1 / M12 / G1a | Represent island, county, MPO and delivery agency separately. Compare projects on Maui, Molokai and Lanai using verified boundaries. Deliberately substitute county geometry for MPO geometry: the wrong planning/approval route must be detected. |
| M10a.1 / M13 | Import the actual signed county agreement, amendments, phase authorization and funding events. A missing MOA, expired authority or expenditure preceding authorization must remain unresolved; an uploaded title page cannot establish agreement readiness. |
| M10c.1 / M11 | Follow one county pay period from underlying cost and payment evidence through returned corrections and reimbursement reconciliation. Distinguish agency payment, eligibility review, state processing and federal reimbursement authorization. Duplicate amounts or an unsupported phase transfer must fail reconciliation. |
| M10d.1 / M4 | Preserve acceptance, final estimate, unresolved claims, property/utility obligations, federal closeout and retention separately. Mark construction accepted while leaving a known property obligation open: project closure must not erase it. |
| M14 / M8b | Bind procurement and DBE requirements to source version, applicable service, funding and date. Challenge with the obsolete threshold, proposed goal and absent approval. The product must explain uncertainty rather than fabricate readiness. |
| M2 / M9 / G1a | Produce a county-to-HDOT handoff with readable evidence, named reviewers and external receipt. Exercise island-specific public participation and accessible alternatives. Exporting a packet alone must not mark agency receipt or approval complete. |

These mechanisms belong in existing projects, funding, procurement, records and planning workflows. A separate Hawaii module would conceal shared problems instead of resolving them.

Full Hawaii review should follow source reconciliation: obtain the current executed county MOAs and amendments; confirm any unpublished/current LPA revisions; reconcile procurement, billing and retention requirements; then read the remaining manual chapters, current forms and applicable transit procedures. A representative county practitioner case should exercise real authority and a correction cycle before claiming procedural coverage. Full national scope remains unchanged; reconnaissance establishes review priorities, not state completion.
