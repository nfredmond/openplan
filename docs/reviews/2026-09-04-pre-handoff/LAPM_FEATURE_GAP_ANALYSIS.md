## What was too vague in the roadmap

The full capital lifecycle, procurement from both sides, contract drawdown, and agency/sponsor reporting were already restored core requirements. The manual exposes obligations those broad headings could still lose:

- **Agency duties beyond one project:** FHWA Title VI/LEP and ADA programs, complaints, training, annual/biennial certification and review; entity-wide audit, exemption, corrective-action and final determination records.
- **After construction:** service-life maintenance, inspection deficiencies, transferred ownership, continuing property/mitigation duties and county-maintained road-mileage certification for funding apportionment.
- **Planning projects without construction:** NI work plans, regional studies with agency cost allocation, ITS classification and FTA transfer paths. A planning study must not require fictitious PS&E or ROW certification.
- **Operational evidence:** authority and agreement versions, separate funding clocks, negotiated/approved cost bases, sealed A&E fees, on-call solicitation caps, parcel/utility cases, independently checked quantities, materials/payroll records, prompt payment and current official invoice/closeout packets.

I am making these explicit within M1/M4/M8/M9/M10/M11/M13/M14. The requirements ledger labels manual-derived acceptance detail separately from a newly recovered historical user request. This is an expansion of specificity; it is not a claim these capabilities were previously implemented.

## Shared mechanisms to build once

**Dated authority and obligation records.** Preserve official source bytes/hash and section, edition, supersession, effective trigger, program/fund/phase, NHS/SHS/ownership and responsible reviewer. Distinguish software permission from an outside agency's approval. An obligation has its own event anchor, recurrence, applicable calendar, deadline/extension and accepted evidence. Source conflicts stay unresolved until a recorded authoritative disposition. A new bulletin identifies affected cases for review without rewriting historic approvals.

**Separate financial events with one source-cost identity.** Authorized budget, commitment, incurred cost, agency payment, consultant billing, funder claim, approval, reimbursement, retainage and recovery are different amounts/states. Store fund/phase/task/employee/deliverable allocations, unit/cost basis, historical rates, eligibility and source records. Noncash match is not cash; an advance is not ordinary reimbursement; master/on-call ceilings cover their combined task orders. Working edits must not rewrite certified invoice support. Agency accounting remains the reconciled system of record where designated.

**Linked specialist cases and field evidence.** Reuse project identity, GIS and Documents for parcel rights/relocation, utilities/railroads, permits/mitigation, contract changes, materials, labor and disputes. Scope changes and expired rights identify affected prerequisites. Field observations, agency quantity sheets and independent checking support payment; photographs or drone outputs alone are not certified quantities. Keep units, location, bid item, pay period, preparer/checker and cumulative reconciliation.

**Protected exact packages and durable transitions.** Published solicitation criteria, sealed fees, signed drawings, hearing records, certified invoices and closeout files retain exact versions. Save/approve/transmit/received/accepted/paid are separate facts with actual receipts. Restrict fee proposals, payroll/interviews, relocation cases, bank details and counsel/CO memoranda at storage/API/search/export/agent boundaries. Public archives are reviewed derivatives of retained authoritative records. Preserve audit/legal/property holds and the correct retention event through backup/restore.

**Recurring work after a project closes.** Agency program years, audit years, road certification, maintenance ownership, inspections and continuing commitments need assigned work and escalation. Extend My Work, Plans/Projects, GIS and Programs. Do not erase duties with a project-complete flag or invent an agency sanction from an overdue app task.
