## Applicability and authority must be modeled first

California geography alone does not establish LAPM applicability. Chapters 13–16 repeatedly distinguish locally administered Federal-aid work off the State Highway System (SHS), work on SHS under agreements and other manuals, funding participation, and particular contract requirements. Federal funding in another phase can still trigger ROW/utility and Buy America duties. NHS, SHS, Federal-aid-system status, ownership and administering agency are different facts. Neither the workspace home geography nor the label “RTPA” supplies these answers. [Ch13 §§13.1–13.2, pp1–7; Ch14 §14.1, p1; Ch15 §15.1, p1; Ch16 §16.1, pp1–2](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch13.pdf)

The project needs an applicability record containing the administering and asset-owning agencies; public employee in responsible charge; relevant delegations and qualified specialists; SHS/NHS status; funded phases; agreement/E-76/authorization versions; contract specifications and date anchors; district routing; effective sources and approved exceptions. Represent unresolved applicability as unresolved. A generic user role cannot establish professional licensure, agency employment or delegated authority to certify a ROW file or accept construction.

The full-time public employee in responsible charge remains necessary even when consultants administer work. Off-SHS the RE need not personally be registered if appropriately supervised by a registered engineer; this is not permission for an unqualified person to make engineering judgments. A project’s RE, responsible-charge employee, independent checker, property specialist, labor officer and funder reviewer need distinct assignments. [Ch16 §§16.1–16.2, pp1–5](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch16.pdf)

Certification states need their own model. Certification 1 concerns completed rights/relocation and necessary arrangements; 2 permits documented possession/entry rights under its conditions. Certification 3 is exceptional and requires a detailed justification and upgrade to 1 or 2 before bid opening, submitted to DLAE at least 15 working days before opening. Certification 3 is not sufficient for the cited CTC allocation purpose. Refresh stale or changed certifications: one year without advertisement, changed PS&E/scope or relevant dates trigger review. [Ch13 §13.10, pp22–26](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch13.pdf)

[Ch13 §13.8, pp15–18](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch13.pdf)

Do not use one “three years after project complete” deletion timer. ## Chapter 14 — Utility cases and authorization

The utility record should be **one case per owner/facility conflict**, with location, affected plan revision, owner claim/property or franchise basis, liability decision, resolution plan and itemized estimate, permit requirements, agreement, notice to owner, authorization, schedule, inspection and final net cost. Each case feeds the clearance memo and ROW certification. Public-serving utility work and private/service-connection cost-to-cure are treated differently. [Ch14 §§14.1–14.2, pp1–4](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch14.pdf)

The ordinary sequence is early owner/map coordination, plotted facilities and conflict investigation, then following NEPA the resolution plan/estimate/claim, engineering review, liability analysis and Report of Investigation 14-E, district review of draft documents, owner concurrence as needed, and Notice to Owner 14-D plus agreement where the agency is liable. The notice identifies facilities, plan and completion terms. Permits precede work. Agreements 14-F/14-G and agency-owned-utility form 14-I have distinct uses. Keep a facility diary throughout. [Ch14 §14.2, pp2–4](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch14.pdf)

For federally participating relocation, E-76 and Specific Authorization 14-C are required before physical work. Third-party utility payments ordinarily belong to ROW; relocation done exclusively by the highway contractor can belong to construction with the specified PS&E/authorization documentation. A locally funded utility phase still needs the applicable utility records without inventing a 14-C requirement. Figure 14-1 shows conditional branches: absence of agency liability does not eliminate documentation, and contractor-performed work changes which records are required. [Ch14 §14.3, pp5–9 and Figure 14-1, printed iii](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch14.pdf)

Record the five-part authorization package, review/return cycles, date and amount limits. Major scope/cost changes require prompt reauthorization; the smaller-change path differs. Nonstandard agreement clauses need HQ/Legal review before execution. The final invoice needs inspection/completion evidence and salvage/betterment or other applicable credits; do not reimburse the gross estimate as though it were actual net cost. [Ch14 §14.3, pp5–8](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch14.pdf)

Unanticipated conflicts need immediate DLAE coordination, possible environmental reevaluation and special authorization **before** work. The chapter permits written or verbal authorization under this exception, followed by documented confirmation and the full package; it is not permission for an agent to approve emergency work. Capture who authorized what and when, the five-working-day written confirmation where applicable, and 30-day package obligation. Full Specific Authorization must precede invoice processing. Positive utility identification is mandatory on SHS; §14.4 distinguishes off-SHS policy and then uses broad safety language. Do not turn that passage into an unqualified universal mandate. [Ch14 §§14.3–14.4, pp8–9](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch14.pdf)

**Home:** Projects → ROW/utilities cases, linked GIS conflicts, Documents, authorization and Funding/Invoicing. **Done:** clearance and each utility claim can be reconstructed from the case; unknown liability or missing authorization cannot become ready. **Verification:** nonparticipating case, contractor-performed case, changed scope, emergency verbal authorization, missing salvage credit, stale plan revision and utility incomplete at ROW certification. Budget for specialist review and external response time; the chapter’s normal review expectation is not a guaranteed turnaround.

Consequential details that a generic checklist misses:

- **Quantity-to-payment lineage:** each Q sheet identifies bid item, station/depth/offset, measurements/calculation, source of each figure, dated preparer and independent checker. Cumulative paid quantity must equal source quantities. The LPA produces the sheet; a contractor invoice is not its substitute. Checks must be completed no later than final payment; the application should make uncleared checks visible during progress review. Force-account bills are per CO/day and reconcile to field labor/equipment/material, payroll, wage/rental rates and invoices. Materials-on-hand has its own contract, value, purchase, storage/control, protection and request conditions. Do not count it again as newly paid when incorporated. (§16.13, pp48–51.)
- **CO control:** the interagency CO memorandum must not be sent to the contractor. Independent estimate and signed/dated preparation/checking differ from accepting the contractor proposal. Agreed-price work should be agreed before performance; if no agreement use the appropriate force-account route. Quantity variation rules have contractual conditions; ±25% is not an automatic universal adjustment. Time impacts require critical-path reasoning and avoidance of double-counted delay. Nonparticipating costs still follow federal contract requirements. (§16.10, pp28–38.)
- **Materials:** contract test methods/versions are fixed by the Notice to Bidders, not silently updated to the latest standard. Changing methods requires CO/justification. Independent-assurance results test the testers and cannot substitute for material acceptance. Certificates are lot/delivery-specific where required; source inspection does not guarantee undamaged, acceptable delivery. Minor-quantity acceptance without testing requires the adopted QAP and all specified conditions, not a broad exemption for a small project. (§16.11, pp39–46.)
- **Labor privacy:** interviews are confidential and conducted away from employer presence; the chapter gives a minimum of two per contract/month and coverage of prime/subcontractors, with continuing checks. Missing-payroll withholding is a refundable document-linked mechanism, not a compounding penalty for the same missing sheet each month. Other penalties require notices and DIR approval. (§16.9, pp17–23.)
- **Environmental continuity:** an ECR entry needs source document/page, execution phase, assigned certifier, date and evidence. Permit expiry, omitted PS&E mitigation, training/monitor requirements and post-construction reports must affect the relevant readiness state. (§16.12, pp47–48.)
- **Claims and closeout:** a dispute is not yet the same as a claim against the proposed final estimate. Contractual notice and claims stages, retained counsel analysis, recovery credits and funding approvals matter. Final inspection, contract acceptance, DLAE verification and funder reimbursement completion are different acts. (§§16.16–16.18, pp54–62.)

## Sequence, definitions of done and verification

1. **Establish applicability, authority and the contract file.** A representative agency bridge project has verified funding/SHS/NHS/administration facts, qualified roles, source versions, agreements and date anchors. Support a local-only and SHS branch without imposing the wrong rules. Unknown authority or policy change keeps affected readiness unresolved. Test wrong district, consultant as public responsible-charge employee, expired qualification and changed contract/source revision. Cost: maintaining official source profiles and professional review; no paid SaaS is inherently required.
2. **Join environmental/ROW/utilities/PS&E to advertisement.** Parcel rights, easement windows, relocation, railroad/utility arrangements and ECR obligations feed a reviewed certification/PS&E baseline. Advertise only against applicable authorization, or a recorded valid exception. Test late easement expiry, certification 3/3W distinction, changed scope, unfinished utility, unresolved owner liability and environmental condition omitted from contract. Cost: sensitive storage, specialist time and external-agency delays.
