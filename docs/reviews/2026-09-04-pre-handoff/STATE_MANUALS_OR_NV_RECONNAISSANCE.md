## Oregon: current chapters, different delivery arrangements

The [official LAG index](https://www.oregon.gov/ODOT/LocalGov/Pages/LAG-Manual.aspx) identifies the current manual as guidance for **certified** local public agencies. It publishes chapters independently: Section A and billing Chapter C3 are November 2025, Section B is November 2021, and environmental Chapter C6 is July 2026. The updated consolidated manual is marked coming soon; the linked full 2021 manual is archived. Do not label that archive the latest operative set.

[Section A](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/LocalGov/Documents/Section-A.pdf), especially A2.2–A3.5, distinguishes delivery by the certified agency itself, another certified agency, ODOT, or a state-fund exchange arrangement. The agreement records the chosen method; several alternatives have guidance outside this certified-agency manual. Certification is function-specific, with MPO certification focused on consultant selection/administration. ODOT retains specified approvals, including final acceptance and ROW/utility certificate co-signing. These distinctions require separate sponsor, delivery agent, responsible employees and retained approvers in OpenPlan.

[Billing Chapter C3](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/LocalGov/Documents/Section-C-Chapter-3.pdf), §§3.1–3.3, distinguishes the certification/project agreements, FHWA funding authorization, expenditure account and ODOT notice to proceed. Bills carry agreement, key/project and account identities. The chapter describes requested billing within 90 days of incurrence and typical final invoices within 45 days after each funding phase, subject to the actual agreement. Department/unit indirect-rate approval is separate from incurred cost and funding available. These are source-specific dates and financial dimensions, not replacements for California's rules.

The [bulletin index](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/LocalGov/Pages/certification-bulletins.aspx) also links a [status summary](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/LocalGov/Documents/Bulletin-Summary.pdf) dated December 24, 2025. Its date predates the July 2026 environmental chapter. It cannot establish that every later update is incorporated. The inspected summary flags several entries as pending update and includes annual reviews; a complete current-bulletin reconciliation remains work before an operative Oregon profile. The errata link was not successfully opened by the web reader.

**Recommendation:** prioritize Oregon for full chapter reading and current bulletin/form reconciliation. Use an MPO consulting-only case and a project delivered by a different certified agency as early architecture tests. The same project record must preserve both agencies' obligations and actual authority without sharing all tenant data or pretending every agency is certified for construction.

## Nevada: useful source, currency unresolved

The accessible [NDOT LPA Manual](https://www.dot.nv.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/1541/636349322493730000) is dated **April 6, 2017**, with 134 PDF pages. Its contents and selected passages establish research leads, not confirmed 2026 requirements. The live design hub and direct local download returned HTTP 403; web extraction of the PDF worked. A bounded search did not establish a newer consolidated edition or complete amendment set.

In this dated text, §6.7.2's ROW certification levels differ from California's numbering and depend partly on prior agency experience/oversight. Chapter 3 separates preliminary/final cooperative agreements and obligations. §§14.7–14.9 route construction invoices through the NDOT RE with monthly quantities, while other phases use the LPA coordinator. Chapter 15 distinguishes final acceptance, audit/balance recovery, agreement closeout and federal voucher. The older audit/de-minimis provisions need current federal/state reconciliation.

**Recommendation:** include Nevada in the next full review, first resolving current authority and supplemental instructions. Namespace certification states by source/profile. Never map California “3” directly onto Nevada “3,” or infer a current deadline from an old PDF still discoverable online.
