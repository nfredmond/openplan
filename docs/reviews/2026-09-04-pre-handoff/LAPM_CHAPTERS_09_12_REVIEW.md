## Findings that should change the roadmap

The current source has useful Projects, work plans, stage gates, document custody, proposal preparation and billing foundations. It does not establish a complete LAPM workflow. Four substantial omissions need explicit acceptance cases inside existing milestones:

1. M8/M9 must cover the **agency's FHWA Title VI and ADA programs**, including recurring reports, complaints and corrective actions. Transit service-equity calculations are a different, narrower job.
2. M14/M11 must cover **on-call procurement and professional-service financial administration**: solicitation-wide ceilings, task competitions, independent estimates, inaccessible fee proposals, accepted indirect rates, scope-bound amendments and notices to proceed.
3. M10/M8 must cover **the transformation of approvals into buildable contract requirements**: every mitigation/permit condition resolves to a plan sheet, specification, cost, responsible person and eventual field evidence.
4. M10/M1 must cover **versioned PS&E and civil-rights applicability**, including separate proposal receipt and bidder data, unchanged FHWA-1273 incorporation, design responsibility, work-zone plans, material provenance and changing effective dates.

These are added detail for the restored full planning/capital/procurement scope. They do not justify separate modules for each chapter or a reduction of the nationwide v1 destination.
