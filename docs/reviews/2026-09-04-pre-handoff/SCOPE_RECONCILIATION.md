## Existing contract and implementation that needed clearer completion criteria

These are obligations supported by the current product contract, prior roadmap and implementation records. The proposed acceptance details are professional/engineering recommendations. We did not recover a direct historical user quote prescribing every editor function, analytical method or statutory workflow below.

- **BCA, transportation demand management and agency prioritization:** M6b and M6c now distinguish a defensible calculation/memo from an agency's project-selection decision. Preserve currency/source years, assumptions, sensitivity, unknowns and approved judgment. Existing saved BCA/TDM results and priority frameworks must be reused. Historical screening claims do not establish funder-specific adequacy.
- **Whole land-use plans and development review:** M7 now expressly requires full plan production and a separate application-to-decision/conditions case. Housing inventories, mapped designations or stored policies cannot close those larger jobs. The ledger names comprehensive/general/community/specific plans, housing, zoning, downtown, parks/open space, historic preservation, design guidelines, annexation and feasibility work.
- **Environmental, climate/resilience and Title VI practice:** M8 names separate applicable study/consultation/decision/mitigation, exposure/action/monitoring and adopted-policy service-equity cases. Generated narrative and translated surveys do not establish those outcomes. Current legal applicability and specialist review remain prerequisites.
- **Shared human documents and plan archives:** M2b and M5 require non-RTP drafting/review and an independently usable plan package, with exact source bytes, approved versions, maps/tables and exclusions. Current document/library foundations exist. The evidence does not require an unrequested live-cursor editor or every vendor DMS connector.
- **Inclusive daily operation:** the shared definition of done retains localization, contrast, print, slow/interrupted connections and usable non-map alternatives throughout the app. This does not promise full offline synchronization without a separate need/design review.
- **Distinct organizations:** the ledger and observation protocol separate small/rural work, tribal authority/data control, statewide distributed review, consulting across clients, nonprofit/community handoff, transit operators, overlapping regional agencies and public participation. A convenient case cannot stand in for every organization.
