# Observe real planning work

This protocol prepares human research recommended by the September 4 review.
No participant has been recruited or observed through this document. Nathaniel
owns recruitment, outreach and the product decisions arising from it. Agent
journeys remain useful regression/discovery evidence, separately labeled.

## Start with work people already need to do

Choose permitted existing records and a useful task before choosing screens.
Use a protected copy where appropriate. Agree on what the participant expects
to produce and who must use it afterward. Do not invent a realistic-looking
project, source result or legal conclusion to get through a broken workflow.

For the first qualitative round, seek these perspectives; one person can cover
more than one context. This is a starting sample, not statistical representation
of planning practice or nationwide v1 proof.

| Participant context | Actual work to observe | What the observation can reveal |
|---|---|---|
| Local agency planner | Take an existing corridor, plan amendment or other active case from intake to review | Whether context, sources and next actions survive module boundaries |
| Consultant serving multiple clients | Prepare and transfer a case whose client geography differs from the office/workspace home | Authority, confidentiality, repeated entry and recipient reuse |
| Small/rural or tribal organization | Complete its own chosen job using its actual sources, authority and connectivity | Capacity assumptions, missing data, sovereign authority and service dependence |
| Reviewer, approver or records steward | Return a record for changes, approve an exact version and prepare its public derivative | Meaning of approval, source completeness, confidentiality and records responsibility |
| GIS/model/data recipient | Open the resulting workbook, GIS and report evidence in their normal tools | Whether units, geometry, version, exclusions and claim limits remain understandable |
| Public participants | Submit input on a permitted real draft and understand receipt, moderation and agency response | Accessibility, language, trust, non-map access and comprehension of influence |
| Capital-project manager, resident engineer and finance reviewer | Take over a permitted bridge or other capital case, prepare a progress payment/reimbursement period and trace outstanding closeout obligations | Actual phase authority, consultant/contractor/funder distinctions, missing evidence, amount reconciliation and field-to-office handoffs |
| Student or solo planner using a personal agent connection | Select an installed CLI or API backend, draft from selected evidence, review one proposed action and recover from an unavailable backend | Installation burden, data/account understanding, source comprehension, unsafe trust and interruption behavior |
| Independent operator/contributor | Install or change a small real function using the documented path | Hidden founder knowledge, recoverability and maintainability |

Do not let a convenient California case stand in for every jurisdiction. Add a
non-California authority, a regional/overlapping case and contexts unlike the
first participants as the product matures. California still requires deeper
sub-state and agency proof; the entire contract remains in force.

## Session preparation

Record the candidate SHA, runtime identity, task scope, participant role/context,
permitted source records, expected deliverable and intended recipient. Explain
what is being observed, what is retained, who can access it and how participation
can stop. Seek permission separately for recordings; observation notes may be
sufficient. Keep identifiable originals and sensitive participant material out
of public repositories and agent histories.

Offer the accessibility and language arrangements the participant needs. Include
keyboard/screen-reader users and mobile/non-map participation as actual tasks,
not a checklist appended after desktop work. Current US state/local government
guidance identifies WCAG 2.1 AA as the web/mobile standard; maintain the applicable
dated requirements for the participating authority. [DOJ guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/small-entity-compliance-guide/).

Nathaniel selects the real planning outcome and the appropriate authority/policy
reviewers. Engineering prepares isolated accounts, a recoverable permitted data
copy, recording boundaries and a tested reset/recovery plan for the research
environment. Do not conduct failure injection against real agency work.

## Conduct the task

Give the participant their goal and initial records, without naming the screens
or prescribing the click sequence. Observe where they begin, what they expect,
what they trust and where they become uncertain. Ask them to explain important
choices in their own words. Avoid coaching until help is needed; record every
intervention and distinguish independent completion from assisted completion.

Include a realistic recoverable interruption or returned review in the isolated
case. For example, resume saved work after a connection loss or respond to a
reviewer who asks for a source or changed assumption. A deliberately unavailable
source must stay unavailable; do not substitute invented measurements.

Have the recipient reopen the actual artifact in their normal tool. Ask what
geography, period, units and sources it represents, what is excluded, and which
claims they could responsibly reuse. For a model comparison, ask the participant
to distinguish the two methods and the declared validation/use boundary. For
public participation, ask what was received, what is public and how the agency
will account for it. An unexplained download or a confident misunderstanding
is a failure worth fixing.

## Record outcomes without a satisfaction score standing in for usefulness

Capture whether the intended outcome was reached independently, reached with
assistance, partly reached or not reached; elapsed task time; failed attempts;
requests for help; repeated entry; lost meaning or work; the artifact recipient's
findings; and the participant's understanding of consequential limits.

Keep observation separate from interpretation. Record what happened, then the
possible cause and evidence needed to distinguish it. A participant's preference
is valuable but not automatically a redesign requirement. A repeated confident
misunderstanding about authority, privacy or scientific validity is consequential
even if participants say they liked the interface.

Use a private research record and a sanitized finding for engineering/product
work. Bind the latter to the candidate, actual task, evidence and an owner. Do
not publish a person's name, voice, disability, agency-sensitive case or comments
without the appropriate permission. Do not turn a small qualitative sample into
a national completion percentage.

## Decide and repeat

Classify the finding as a proven defect, unsupported claim, design question or
participant preference. Bring Nathaniel the decisions about planning outcomes,
authority/policy expectations and priority. Engineering chooses the mechanism
and meaningful regression tests. Amend the single roadmap when the evidence
changes sequencing; retain the full v1 destination.

Repeat the affected job after a correction and retain the original result. An
agent regression can protect a repaired interaction, but it does not replace
the participant/recipient check that the task now makes sense. Independent
installation, complete restore, public accessibility and nationwide scientific
acceptance each require their own evidence beyond this first qualitative round.

## Compare engagement outcomes with Social Pinpoint

A claim of superiority requires access to a current lawful comparison environment and a fair task protocol. This review used competitor documentation, not a live comparison. Before recruiting, choose comparable campaign questions, geometry/data complexity, participant tasks and agency administration tasks; record versions and predeclare success/error/effort measures. Counterbalance platform order and record assistance. Include meaningful geometry entry through touch and keyboard/non-map paths, receipt/privacy understanding, moderation and a response tied to a decision.

Measure both participant comprehension/completion and staff time to publish, moderate, interpret, respond and export. Preserve minority/conflicting contributions when assessing synthesis. Qualitative observation can identify problems and promising improvements, but a small convenient sample does not establish representative opinion or a universal market-superiority claim. If comparator access is unavailable, publish that limit and validate OpenPlan's outcomes without claiming the head-to-head comparison passed.

## Contract review and complete RTP update cases

Observe a consulting PM, staff member and finance reviewer performing a weekly review on a permitted planning agreement: actual work, remaining effort, deliverable readiness, review deadlines and one emerging fee/date risk. Require independent figure reconstruction. Include a task split across staff/deliverables, amended rates/scope, nonbillable rework and an incomplete historical opening balance. Evaluate entry burden and whether warnings lead to a useful decision; automated arithmetic correctness alone does not answer that.

Observe an RTP lead starting with the prior adopted plan, recovering its figures/tables/chapters with citations, resolving a conflict, updating policy/action/financial elements and taking a draft through public review, response and adoption preparation. A regional finance/programming reviewer checks the actual financial tables; the plan approver checks the exact packet and limitations. This is a different budget from the consultant fee for preparing the RTP. Do not treat an upload/extraction success as a completed update or simulated participants as research.

## Municipal tax and grant reporting case

Observe two independent municipal preparers/certifiers and an administrator program/finance reviewer using one permitted reporting cycle. A city submits project quantities and expenditures, receives a correction request, revises and certifies. The administrator accepts the report and separately records an authorized formula distribution or reimbursement. An oversight/public reader must understand quantities, spending and missing coverage without seeing private attachments.

Use a Napa-style local-return case and a separately documented allocated grant to test differing payment/report rules. Independently reconcile quantities and balances, including cumulative updates, the same asset funded by two sources, a no-activity report, a missing report and a successor-measure obligation. Validate the actual agency forms, workflow burden, certification vocabulary and reporting calendar. Agent simulations cannot establish municipal adoption or legal/financial adequacy.

## Agency solicitation and consultant response

Observe an agency planner/procurement reviewer and a separate consultant pursuit lead with approved real historical material or a clearly marked test solicitation. Follow an actual planning assignment from source discovery and agency scope/criteria preparation through questions, an addendum, response, receipt, evaluation and signed-work handoff. Run a qualifications RFQ and a price-inclusive RFP separately under reviewed applicable rules. Do not contact a live issuer or submit a real bid as a test without explicit authority.

Ask participants to identify what remains missing, which deadline/zone controls, which files will be sent, what the receipt proves, who may view costs and what authorizes work to begin. Observe a changed notice, missing attachment, interrupted upload, conflict/recusal and protest hold. Check desktop, 390px, keyboard, assistive technology and actual document outputs. Compare agency/consultant effort and mistakes with their real current process; simulated completion cannot establish procurement usefulness. Transfer the accepted scope, rates, tasks, deliverables and deadline into the M11 contract case without concealing negotiated changes.

## Remote trial and customer ownership

Use a fresh browser on a network independent of the development computer. Nathaniel can demonstrate the product, but then an agency/consultant participant must log in and complete an allowed real planning task without hidden shell operations. Include saving, returning from another browser, an upload, an actual background job, a report and an invitation/password-reset message. Record unfinished capability and scientific limitations; do not use precomputed results as evidence that a fresh job ran.

Next, move the participant's authorized trial to a separate customer-controlled installation. An administrator unfamiliar with the original setup must recover the data, verify roles and continue work using documentation. Observe both paid implementation handoff and the independent self-host path. Explain which support is optional, what third-party costs remain, how upgrades/backups work and how to stop the service without losing access to software or records. Hosting budgets and new accounts require separate authorization before these live exercises.
