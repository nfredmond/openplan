## Findings that determine the design

The two sides need a shared solicitation identity and separate private working records. An agency's procurement file, each firm's proposal workspace, and the formally received submission serve different people and have different access rules. A generated PDF is not evidence of publication or receipt. A ranking is not a contract award, and an award is not permission to start work.

The label "planning services" cannot select a legal workflow. The scope, construction relationship, funding participation, procuring entity, state law and agency procedures determine whether qualifications-based selection, a price-inclusive proposal process, quotations or a justified exception applies. Likewise, RFQ can mean qualifications or quotations. Store the full method and rules, not an ambiguous acronym.

Electronic cost concealment is a material requirement for applicable A&E selection. Its implementation must cover storage access, document previews, extraction, indexing, exports and assistant context. Hiding a price field from a scoring screen would leave several ways to compromise the process. This is an engineering implication of the sources below, not an observed OpenPlan defect.

## Applicability before templates

FHWA explains that federal-aid-funded consultant services use the federal grant framework; engineering/design services leading directly to construction also invoke the highway A&E rules. Its Q&A distinguishes broad corridor/regional assessments and travel-demand studies from analyses advancing a particular construction project. State/local professional-service laws can still require qualifications-based selection even when federal Brooks Act requirements do not. A planning title alone settles neither classification. [FHWA overview](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/programadmin/consultant.cfm), [Q&A, competitive negotiation question 4, PDF pp.6–7](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/programadmin/172qa.pdf).

California §4525 defines covered services and entities. Section 4526 requires competence/qualifications-based selection for the listed professional services, including environmental services, and addresses conflicts. Section 4528's mandatory sequential negotiation wording applies to state agency heads; its local-agency paragraph is permissive. Other controlling requirements can independently mandate that sequence. Do not tell every local planner that §4528 alone imposes the state process. [§4525](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=4525.), [§4526](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=4526.), [§4528](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=4528).

The generic grant rule distinguishes states/tribes from other recipients. Where §200.320 applies, competitive proposals generally consider price and other factors; its price-excluding qualifications method is limited to A&E professional services, not everything an A&E firm sells. Section 200.319 also excludes specification/scope drafters from competing on those procurements. DOT §1201.317 expressly modifies the generic rule for state subrecipients, permitting state-allowed policies. Funding agency and pass-through relationship therefore matter. [2 CFR §§200.317–.320](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/subtitle-A/chapter-II/part-200), [DOT §1201.317](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/subtitle-B/chapter-XII/part-1201/section-1201.317).

LAPM separates federal A&E, state-only A&E and non-A&E. Its non-A&E examples include outreach, traffic counts, speed surveys and local roadway safety plans. Selected A&E procedures require documented receipt, published evaluation criteria, distributed written answers/addenda and concealed electronic cost proposals inaccessible to procurement participants. Unsuccessful cost proposals also remain protected from the storage provider. Selection leads into negotiation, execution, administration and records. These are distinct stages, not one upload-and-score action. [Chapter 10, printed pp.24–32, 40–45, 57–62](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/local-assistance/documents/lapm/ch10.pdf).

Under the federal A&E method, the regulation calls for fair public solicitation, disclosed criteria, price exclusion from ranking, an independent estimate before reviewing the selected firm's cost, and ordered negotiation. It establishes response-time and limited-competition provisions rather than one unconditional bidder-count rule. Conflicts, eligibility checks and procurement documentation also matter. Section 172.9 distinguishes project-specific, multiphase and on-call contracts and their administration. [23 CFR §§172.7–.9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-172).

A nationwide rule record should identify the legal procuring entity and signatory authority, applicable geography, source/version, award and pass-through chain, service classification, approved method, threshold basis, calendar, disclosures and exception approvals. An MPO designation or workspace home county cannot supply those facts. FDOT, for example, points local agencies to Florida §287.055, its LAP procedures and a professional-services checklist alongside federal authorities. All 50 states and DC remain the product ambition; this review establishes California depth and the need for independently reviewed jurisdiction configurations. [FDOT](https://www.fdot.gov/fpo/lp/federal-programs/lap/professional-services-procurement).

## Current-template and disclosure traps

Caltrans currently describes suspended DBE goal-setting/counting pending required reevaluation, with continuing contract duties. The August 2026 forms page marks proposal/contract DBE commitment forms inactive and lists an updated A&E agreement exhibit. Preserve historical requirements on historical procurements; refresh the operative program and forms before creating a new one. Do not automatically impose an old goal or erase all ongoing obligations. This report does not adjudicate an individual firm's eligibility. [Current DBE status](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/local-assistance/local-civil-compliance/disadvantaged-business-enterprise-dbe), [forms index](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/local-assistance/forms/local-assistance-procedures-manual-forms).

PCC §10344 provides different price-inclusive selection approaches for covered state contracts, rejects late receipt, and makes proposals/scoring sheets inspectable after committee scoring under subsection c. Section 10345 addresses proposed-award posting and protests, including a five-working-day posting provision for §10344 awards. These are source-bound examples, not universal deadlines for every MPO, RTPA, municipality or method. [§10344](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PCC&sectionNum=10344.), [§10345](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PCC&sectionNum=10345.).

Public-record handling needs a decision record, not a blanket "confidential proposal" checkbox or automatic publication of every attachment. California requires justification for withholding and recognizes applicable protections in other law. A vendor's requested redactions should be preserved for review without being treated as the agency's legal determination. [§7922.000](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=7922.000.), [§7927.705](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=7927.705.).

Two planning-agency examples make the delivery problem concrete. NCTC's 2025 transit operational-analysis solicitation required physical bound copies and USB delivery, a separate questions deadline and shared answers. Its posted deadline has passed; it must not appear as a currently open opportunity. MTC describes a portal supporting opportunity documents/events/deadlines, registered submission and closed/awarded records. Neither example makes an OpenPlan export a valid submission to that agency. [NCTC](https://www.nctc.ca.gov/RFP/), [MTC](https://www.mtc.ca.gov/jobs-contracts/contract-vendor-opportunities).

## Proposed complete workflow

The following are product requirements and engineering proposals derived from the reviewed needs. They are not additional statutory mandates or descriptions of current OpenPlan behavior.

| Job | Agency outcome | Consultant outcome and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Identify the need | Connect adopted work program, RTP/plan/project, geography, budget, fund conditions and staff capacity to a procurement decision | See actual assignment, issuer, method, eligibility, budget disclosure and source freshness; make a recorded pursue/decline decision |
| Author the package | Build scope/tasks, usable deliverables, schedule, proposal instructions, criteria, agreement and required forms; obtain procurement/legal/finance review | Extract a requirements checklist linked to exact clauses; identify unclear requirements and missing materials without inventing answers |
| Publish and monitor | Approve an immutable issued version; record publication location, time, evidence and intended recipients | Discover a deduplicated opportunity; save issuer URL and package version; distinguish open, changed, closed, cancelled and unknown |
| Questions and addenda | Route questions to authorized staff; approve common answers; version amendments and any deadline changes; retain delivery evidence | Prepare authorized questions, receive revisions, compare changes, acknowledge required addenda and update the response checklist |
| Prepare the response | Provide a stable intake specification and any permitted clarification route | Reuse truthful firm qualifications, named team/subconsultants, availability, work samples and project approaches; draft scope, schedule and cost where permitted; review signatures, assumptions and exceptions |
| Submit and receive | Receive a frozen payload and give a durable receipt; distinguish timeliness, successful storage and responsiveness | Authorize final dispatch; retain exact files, addendum acknowledgments, identity, timestamp and agency receipt; an external/physical delivery remains unconfirmed without evidence |
| Evaluate | Assign cleared evaluators; preserve individual scores, cited reasons, interviews and reference checks; produce the permitted ranking | Track receipt, clarification, shortlist and interview status without access to competitors or private panel deliberations |
| Negotiate and decide | Open only permitted cost material; document negotiation, termination/escalation and required approvals; handle notice/protest before final award | Respond to the authorized negotiation stage, retain revisions and understand whether selection, intent, award or execution has occurred |
| Contract and deliver | Transfer signed scope, rates, ceiling, funding, milestones and deliverables into project/contract administration; record notice to proceed | Start authorized work, submit deliverables/invoices, track acceptance, changes, payment and closeout; preserve proposal-to-contract differences |
| Account publicly | Build a reviewable procurement file and appropriately redacted public record with correction history | Receive permitted debriefing and protest outcomes; retain the firm's evidence and lessons without using competitors' protected information |

Keep bid solicitation separate from a grant opportunity and a consultant contract separate from a subrecipient award. Reuse document, evidence, contact, project and task capabilities where the parent's code inventory supports them. A procurement relationship must cross agency/firm boundaries without granting the other party access to the full workspace. Cross-installation exchange needs durable identities, authenticated delivery and matching receipts.

The assistant can suggest task language, detect omissions and organize evidence. It must not invent firm experience, staff consent, professional licenses, funding, signature authority, publication, receipts or award decisions. It should cite the issuer's exact requirement when flagging a response gap. No assistant may see sealed cost data through a shared knowledge index or turn unpublished agency material into consultant advice. Human evaluators remain responsible for judgments; generated scoring explanations must not be passed off as their recorded reasons.

## Milestones and falsifiable acceptance

1. **Authority and package preparation.** Implement reviewed rule/version selection and both authoring workspaces. Accept when an agency can prepare a complete transit/RTP/corridor assignment and a consultant can produce a requirements-linked response from real approved firm material. Demonstrate that unclear A&E classification stays unresolved, changed funding triggers review, and a stale form is identified. This milestone alone does not establish publication or submission.
2. **Issue, amend, submit and preserve receipt.** Complete one agency-issued planning solicitation with separate consultant users, one meaningful addendum, a changed deadline, replacement/withdrawal under the configured rules and final receipt. Prove public/private separation, exact package identity and recovery after interrupted upload. Include an external-portal or physical-delivery case whose status remains unconfirmed until actual evidence is recorded. No real publication or proposal transmission is authorized by this research assignment.
3. **Select using the correct method.** Run separately reviewed QBS and non-A&E price-inclusive cases. Preserve panel conflicts/recusals, independent assessments and final reasons. For QBS, demonstrate that price cannot affect ranking and only the proper negotiation stage releases the permitted cost package. For the other method, demonstrate that published price treatment actually affects the result. Cover inadequate competition and documented exception handling.
4. **Award, protest and contract handoff.** Complete applicable notices, an actual test protest/hold/resolution, approvals, execution and notice to proceed in a controlled acceptance environment. Transfer a planning contract into delivery tracking with one accepted deliverable, an invoice review and a controlled amendment. Generate an audit file and a public version that reconcile to the same procurement history.

Acceptance must use identified-checkout desktop and 390px journeys, keyboard and screen-reader tasks, document/export inspection, console review and role-specific sessions. Include an agency planner, procurement reviewer and consultant preparing an actual planning response; their task is to finish the work and explain its state without developer guidance. No elapsed-time target is invented here. Record completion, unresolved requirements and human errors before claiming usefulness.

Register adversarial cases before implementation: unauthorized cost retrieval through API/storage/export/search/assistant; internal scoring leaked to a proposer; cross-firm proposal access; altered files after receipt; client-clock deadline bypass; incomplete upload acknowledged as complete; daylight-saving or timezone ambiguity; silently edited issued criteria; missed addendum; wrong solicitation destination; duplicated retry; revoked authority still publishing; conflicted evaluator still scoring; protest bypass; intent-to-award treated as execution; an unsigned contract marked ready to start; fabricated staff experience; protected attachment in public export. Guards need targeted failing mutations and a surviving no-op. A passing unit test is not proof that the whole procurement was completed.

## Unresolved before operational use

- Determine the particular agency's procurement authority, award conditions, adopted manual, local protest/public-record rules and approval/signature delegations. A statewide professional-service statute does not fill every procedural gap.
- Obtain full current forms and examine their fields, notices and confidentiality handling during implementation. The index review did not validate every 2026 exhibit or every superseding bulletin.
- Refresh thresholds and program requirements when configuring a live procurement. This report deliberately supplies no universal dollar threshold, price weight, protest duration, retention period or bid-count requirement.
- Review grant-specific FTA, other federal-agency and tribal rules when those funding/authority paths are implemented. FHWA/DOT findings are not a complete substitute. Florida was sampled, not certified as fully covered.
- Validate electronic-signature, receipt-custody, retention, legal-hold and authorized-disposition behavior with the responsible agency. Hashes help detect changed files; they do not establish a lawful signature or prove an unopened database by themselves.
- Current OpenPlan reuse, gaps and milestone placement await the parent's source synthesis. No finding here establishes that existing procurement functionality is absent, secure, legally compliant or accepted.
