## Architecture decision for the roadmap

Extend the consultant pursuit/workspace, firm evidence, Documents, Projects and contract administration. Add an agency-owned procurement case where the bounded inventory establishes no coherent existing record. Share public solicitation identity and reviewed interchange; keep each firm's private preparation, the agency's private working file and formally received submissions separate. An agency adviser that also bids creates a conflict requiring an explicit rule and recusal/exclusion decision. Ordinary workspace membership is insufficient for these boundaries.

Use a method/authority record with the actual legal procuring entity, funding/pass-through chain, service classification and dated agency rules. RFP/RFQ is not enough: RFQ may mean qualifications or quotation. The agency research identifies distinct qualifications and price-inclusive paths; no universal legal threshold, protest duration, retention period or procurement method is assumed. Root independently checked January 2026 Caltrans Chapter 10's electronic-cost handling and non-A&E examples against a fresh PDF text extraction. Applicable cost isolation must cover storage, API, search, OCR, exports and assistant context. Select a reviewed technical design for that boundary; do not invent cryptography or promise an ordinary document bucket supplies it.

The existing Grants.gov adapter is funding discovery, not public contracting coverage. Start procurement discovery with manual official-source intake and a verified optional SAM adapter, then maintained state/local sources with honest coverage and refresh state. The primary research recommends OCDS for reviewed public interchange; it does not supply sealed receipt, identity, evaluation or payment. No reviewed OSS package establishes a drop-in full planning-procurement solution. Preserve free local use and no-model search/authoring; real agency intake needs measured uptime, storage, authoritative time, recovery and operator responsibility.

Award handoff carries executed scope, approved rates/ceiling, task/deliverable assignments, deadlines, funding and notice to proceed into M11 planning contract controls and M10 capital delivery. Preserve differences between proposed, negotiated, signed and amended work. Keep grants/subrecipient awards and consultant procurements distinct.

## Test and acceptance implications

No passing test was removed or altered during this extension. The source review did not execute these suites; historical test claims remain historical.

Include meaningful mutations and a harmless surviving mutation.
