## Defect and scope

The distributed work-loading card previously read audit numbers without checking their recorded hash. A temporary copy with the original work total removed displayed zero, while downloading that same audit rejected its changed bytes. The October 7 reproduction confirmed the September 4 source-review finding. No current corruption in the committed study is claimed.

The reader now verifies audit and comparison logical-byte hashes before display. It checks method/geography identity, audit/input bindings, summary agreement, required numeric evidence, separate method coverage and overall candidate disposition. Missing values cannot become zero. Artifact reads stay inside this published study directory. The card shows an unavailable message when loading or verification fails.

The frozen development artifacts remain unchanged. This correction does not establish model accuracy, production custody ingestion, SQL metadata integrity or independent scientific acceptance. Loading-file bytes retain their existing download verification; the card checks their binding but does not load the entire demand input merely to display its filename/hash. Other published study readers and report/assistant custody consumers need their own verification.
