# CMF Clearinghouse entries cited in the countermeasure list

Each row is one CMF Clearinghouse entry that a `cmfNote` in
`fhwa-proven-safety-countermeasures.v1.json` names. Every entry was opened
at its own detail page on the date in the last column. The middle column
copies the fields the entry page gives, in the Clearinghouse's own words
("et al." included). OpenPlan applies none of these factors by itself:
a planner chooses one that fits the site.

`src/test/countermeasure-cmf-notes.test.ts` checks that every id a note
names has a row here and every row here is named by a note.
