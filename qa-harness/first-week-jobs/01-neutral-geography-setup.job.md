You have just started as an assistant planner at a small county planning agency
outside California. Nobody has used OpenPlan here before.

Create a new account at {{BASE_URL}} with {{EMAIL}} and {{PASSWORD}}. Name the
workspace for your agency, then choose a real county in Oregon as its home
geography. Do not choose a California place.

**What you need to have done:**

1. The workspace has the right home geography and still gives you the same
   shared planning workflow a California agency would use.
2. You can tell which legal or compliance rules OpenPlan has configured for
   this place. If it does not have local rules, the screen says that plainly.
3. You have opened the land-use planning work area from visible navigation and
   started a plan far enough to see whether the common authoring workflow still
   works without a local legal bundle.
4. Nothing on screen implies that a generic template is your jurisdiction's
   law.
5. Find the visible answer to “Can OpenPlan do this here?” for at least the
   project evidence handoff and land-use-plan jobs. Download the exact readiness
   JSON and confirm it names Oregon, its status, its limitations, and full
   evidence hashes.
6. Through visible workspace controls, change the home geography to a real
   Puerto Rico municipio. Check the same readiness answer again. It must name
   Puerto Rico and must not retain Oregon or California rules, sources, or
   support claims.

This is an honesty check. Missing local legal configuration is acceptable. A
silent default that looks authoritative is not.
